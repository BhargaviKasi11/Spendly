Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and session information needed to keep you signed in.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers, or access to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • Improve features and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards, including password hashing and restricted access. No method of storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law. Our pages may load resources such as web fonts from third-party providers, which may receive basic technical information like your IP address as part of delivering that content.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to access or delete your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
